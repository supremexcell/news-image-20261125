## Heroes

### 1. CIA and the Director of National Intelligence hit by a cyberattack
- CNN reported 25 Nov, citing people familiar with the matter: the CIA
  and the Office of the Director of National Intelligence appear to have
  been hit by a cyberattack affecting classified information.
- The intrusion is the latest in a series of cybersecurity incidents and
  technology disruptions hitting the US government and private sector;
  sources say it does not appear related to the cyberattack on the FBI's
  nationwide criminal-information database.
- Sources: CNN via stockbiz.vn 25 Nov roundup.

### 2. Iran says a container ship hit a mine in the Strait of Hormuz
- Iran's Revolutionary Guards say an unidentified container ship struck a
  naval mine and stopped near the Strait of Hormuz on 24 Nov, north of
  the Omani port of Khasab. The vessel's identity, condition and crew
  status remain unknown; no UKMTO warning matches it — the account
  remains an attributed Iranian claim, and the image says "Iran says".
- US Central Command rejects the earlier IRGC claim that three tankers
  hit mines, saying the primary shipping routes are cleared and free of
  mines and still in use by commercial vessels. Neither side's account
  has been independently verified.
- Brent crude settled $2.03 higher at $91.40 per barrel (+2.27%).
- Sources: DeepDraft SITREP 25 Nov; UK Fact Check / National Security
  Journal via the Jerusalem Post; Reuters; The Maritime Executive.

## Grid

### 3. Huawei guilty on 10 of 12 counts
- A US jury in New York found Huawei guilty on 10 of 12 criminal counts,
  including bank fraud, conspiracy to steal trade secrets and conspiracy
  to defraud the US government, after a three-month trial. Verdict
  Tue 24 Nov, first reported 25 Nov. Huawei rejected the verdicts.
- Sources: US Department of Justice via AFP; Financial Times.

### 4. WHO evacuates Ebola hospital staff in DR Congo
- WHO Director-General Tedros Adhanom Ghebreyesus said health workers
  were evacuated from a hospital in Akwe, Ituri province, after
  sheltering there for three days to escape fierce fighting. The Ebola
  response team has been temporarily relocated until the fighting subsides.
  The outbreak totals 5,775 deaths among 11,762 cases.
- Sources: WHO via stockbiz.vn 25 Nov roundup.

### 5. Nvidia's Huang apologises to staff; shares slide 5.9%
- Jensen Huang addressed Nvidia staff at an all-hands meeting a day after
  his reinstatement as CEO; he apologised to employees. Shares fell 5.86%
  to $155.38 on Tue 24 Nov after Monday's +31.5% surge; Anthropic fell
  below its $83 IPO price to $81.90.
- Sources: Investors.com; Motley Fool; Investopedia (25 Nov reporting).

### 6. Real Madrid rout PSV 5-1
